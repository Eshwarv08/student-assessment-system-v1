  6 | Assessment Task Description
  7 | For this assessment, you are working as a telco technician. You have been assigned a task by your supervisor to prepare the site for access and hauling
  8 | of a new 50 pair cable to replace an existing cable between two pits adjacent to a manhole, and installing a new pit, a prefabricated manhole, in
  9 | preparation for jointing. The supervisor has advised that this area is known for gas and that the adjacent manhole and the pits should be opened to
 10 | allow the ducts to vent. You are also required to check for gas as per your workplace guidelines.
 11 | 
 12 | Prior to commencing the task, you are required to assess the work site and complete a Job Safety Analysis (JSA) to capture and addressed hazards,
 13 | unwanted events and potential risks for the job.
