Unit Summary
                          This unit describes the skills and knowledge required to install and test optical fibre cable on a
                          customer's premises for communications applications using a range of terminations that may
                          include direct termination, fusion splicing and mechanical splicing.
Target Group
                          It applies to technical staff who place, secure and terminate optical fibre cable in new installations
                          and upgrades or maintain existing networks in domestic, commercial and industrial installations.
                          Communications applications include digital and analogy, telephony, data, video, digital
                          broadcasting, computer networks, local area networks (LAN), wide area networks (WAN) and
                          multimedia.

                           ASSESSMENT TASK 3 – OBSERVATION
                                                      Fusion Splice
This assessment task requires candidate to demonstrate a fusion splice.

The assessor will demonstrate the appropriate process, then ask candidates to:

AT3.1 position and install cable according to agreed route

AT3.2 Comply with manufacturer’s specifications for installation and industry safety standards

AT3.3 prepare and splice optical fibre following the correct procedure:

    •    Position heat shrink splice protector over one end of the fibre
    •    Strip fibre for 20mm using appropriate fibre stripping tools
    •    Clean fibre with lint free wipe tissues and isopropyl alcohol(IPA fluid)
    •    Ensure fibre is dry before inserting into the splicer
    •    Cleave fibre to give a perpendicular mirror smooth end face cut
    •    10 to 12 mm for 40 mm splice protectors or 15 to 17 mm 60 mm splice protectors
    •    Splice the fibres in auto mode to align fibre cores automatically
    •    Check the quality of the cleaved fibre end face (the splicer estimates cleaved angle for both fibre end faces)
    •    Check LCD display for shadow, bubble, ballooning, necking across the joint

AT3.4 Check loss acceptance level is less than0.05db (re-splice if over)

AT3.5 Place the heat shrink on one end of the fibre and perform splice

AT3.6 Seal the heat shrink protector using splicer heater

AT3.7 Select a suitable testing device and record test results following manufacturer instructions

Required Documents and Equipment
    •    Cable stripping tool
    •    Various samples of cable connectors
    •    Tight buffered pigtail connectors
    •    Heat shrink splice protectors
    •    Lint free wipe tissues and isopropyl alcohol (IPA fluid)
    •    PPE for working with cable
    •    Fusion splicing equipment
    •    Field termination connector
    •    High precision fibre cleaver
    •    Manufacturer’s guidelines/specifications for all tools and machinery.

AT4.8Update records for site and notify client of completion of work.
